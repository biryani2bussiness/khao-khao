Privacy Policy

KhaoKhao uses the information you provide to process food orders, deliver them, manage saved addresses and respond to support requests.

Information collected

Google account identity, name, mobile number, delivery address, building and room details, GPS coordinates when permitted, orders, payment status, support requests and a numerical rating. If you choose Notify Me, we store your contact details and delivery location to let you know when delivery becomes available.

We count visits, cart additions and successful order requests using a random daily browser-session identifier for aggregate business analytics. If you enable notifications, we store your browser's push subscription to send order and support updates.

How information is used

We use Google sign-in for account access, Cloudflare for hosting and order storage, Geoapify for optional address lookup, and WhatsApp for order communication. These services process information according to their respective policies. The merchant uses delivery details to fulfill orders. We do not display your contact details publicly.

Your choices

Location and notification permissions can be withdrawn in device or browser settings. You can manage saved addresses in your profile and clear local app data. To request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of your delivery-interest registration, contact us on WhatsApp. Order records may be retained as needed for business and applicable recordkeeping requirements.
