Terms & Conditions

Ordering and delivery

Menu browsing is available publicly. Orders are subject to delivery-location eligibility, restaurant confirmation and item availability. The merchant currently handles deliveries within its pilot service area. Registering interest does not place an order or guarantee future service.

Pricing and payment

Cash on delivery is the current payment method. Food prices, applicable tax, rounding and delivery fees are shown before confirmation. Packaging is free; delivery is free for discounted food subtotals of ₹150 or more and ₹30 for smaller orders. There is no minimum order value.

Changes, cancellations and concerns

Use the order support or cancellation-request option, then contact the restaurant on WhatsApp for confirmation. The restaurant will confirm whether a change is possible. For a missing, incorrect or unsatisfactory order, contact us on WhatsApp with your order reference. Your statutory rights are not affected.
